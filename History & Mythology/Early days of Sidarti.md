The [[Elves]] found themselves in the desert of [[Sidarti]] after a long march and found the last living [[Jannah]], ancient teachers and being of unimaginable knowledge, guardians of the [[Kianemos|Weave]] and of all creatures those with the strongest connection to the elemental magic. Not knowing the [[Kheioi]]' destruction of the Jannah and usurping of their domaine, it didn't take the elves long to connect with the Jannah and forge alliances and cities with them. 
There where 5 tribes of Jannah left and the elves slowly adjusted and got closer to the elements themselves, each to the Jannah and whose culture (and more) they spent the most time with.
These elements were Water, Air, Fire, Earth and the weave itself. (I know, very original xD) 
And for a time they lived together in harmony. 

However, when [[the Great River Mother]], the Kheioi who created the elves, returned and found her lost children, she saw that they had entered connections of various kinds with the Jannah and fearing for her worship, she sought to disrupt and destroy this connection. She took control of the live giving river, made it her domaine and invited all elves back to her shores, if and only if they forsake and abandoned the Jannah. Many heeded her call, but just as many did not. They thought she couldn't stop them from accessing the water, but they were wrong. For she controlled the tides and the creatures that housed in the river. Many elves fell to flooding or in the jaws of hippos and other creatures.

The Jannah and their five tribes meanwhile dispersed into the desert. The tribe of Fire went into the Mountains of Ore and with their mastery of heat and lifebringing fire, they were able to create the best metals of Sidarti. The tribe of water went into the two Oasis to the north and with their master over nurturing water they made the oasis prosper and perfected husbandry and farming. The tribe of air went into the slicing wastes to the south and joined a nomadic life, with their mastery over ever changing air they became great musicians and artists, but also deadly duelists. The tribe of Stone went into the city of stone, a former Jannah city and with their mastery over sturdy and steadfast stone they expanded their city and still trade with the kingdom to this day. The tribe of the weave went into the area that by now is the scar and with their mastery over the iridescent weave they created the most beautiful creations. 

The Great River Mother meanwhile, even with regaining much of her children, was envious still and so she struck against the Jannah and the heretic elves. In a swift attack she got many of her siblings to help her and still spent alot of her limited energy and annihilated the tribe of the weave, turning their land into a permanent scar in the landscape, from whence twisted and corrupted creatures spill forth. With that tribe of Jannah gone, her mastery over the weave was unparallelled in her realm.
