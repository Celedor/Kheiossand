Centuries ago during the [[The Primordial Wars]] and the [[Age of Strife - Age of Heroes|Age of Strife]] [[The Unapproachable Wing]] created the [[Avians]]. However, they became increasingly paranoid during the years of suffering and bloodshed. As such they did not trust the other [[Kheioi]], nor their creations. In an effort to protect their young creation, they took the [[Dislodged Isles|Isles]] where the created their children und ripped them out of the ocean, in order to place them up into the sky and suspend them there indefinitely. While they succeeded in their plan, it cost them most of their energy, magical and otherwise and so they fell into a deep slumber; still alive but unable to experience the world around them or interact with it. Over the years they slowly became mummified in order to protect their body. Unbeknownst to the Avians living in the floating islands, the Unapproachable Wing is still alive to this day, passively keeping the Isles high in the skies with their power.
