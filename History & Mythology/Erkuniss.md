(prenntic: Er- Night, Kuniss - gathering)

Celebrates the beginning of the darker half of the year. In it villages and cities come together and have large feasts and celebrations, awaiting the sunrise in the morning. It oftentimes includes one (or multiple) large outdoors feasts in city-/village-squares and Kuhli - or blessings for protection, warmth and light. The feasts are oftentimes around a large central fire like bonfires.
Many people also use the day to memorate their deceased relatives and friends, especially those lost in the past year.
