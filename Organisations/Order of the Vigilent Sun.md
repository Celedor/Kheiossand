Name: The Order of Vigilent Sun
Affiliations: [[Church of the Righteous Sun]]
Grandmaster: X
Members: around 300, exact number unknown

The Order of the Vigilent Sun is not the biggest, nor the most important Order of the Church. However, it is still very large and very important. It is mostly responsible for the defense of the Church' border. As such it has also grown steadily since the beginning of the civil war and is oftentimes not sent into battles.

Emblem is a sun on a shield
