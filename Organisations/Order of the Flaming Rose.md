Name: The Order of the Flaming Rose 
Affiliations: [[Church of the Righteous Sun]], The Order of the Iron Fist, the Duchy of Zeich Administration. 
Grandmaster: Clovis Gros 
Members: Around 40, however exact number is known to few due to the nature of the order. 

One of the oldest, yet smallest Orders that have been founded in the early years of [[Prennso|Prenntic]] expansion to its current southern border and the establishment of Duchy of Zeich. Covered in mysteries and secrecies, the order started as an investigation force by the church into [[the Vast Canopy ]]besetting the empire from the south. Overtime, given lack of progress with the Vast Canopy and the growing animosity towards the mages, the order effectively reformed into a mage hunting order, tending to their new duty ever since. 
Over the last century, the Order of the Flaming Rose has existed in a strange symbiosis with the much newer and larger order - the Order of the Iron Fist. While the Iron Fist tends to the public needs and administration concerning public safety and church's image in the Duchy of Zeich, the Flaming Rose works behind the scenes, hunting and eliminating rogue mages and other threats of Kianemos origins. 
The Order of the Flaming Rose operates in small, independent cells and has no public face of its own, instead utilizing Iron Fist's infrastructure when needed. All cells answer directly to grandmaster and have little to no communication or knowledge of each other, making the order extremely secretive in both its workings, resources and members. A complete picture is known to only the Grandmaster - Clovis Gros himself and perhaps a few of his close subordinates.
