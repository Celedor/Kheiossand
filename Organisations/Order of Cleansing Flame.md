Name: The Order of the Cleansing Flame 
Affiliations: [[Church of the Righteous Sun]]
Grandmaster: X
Members: around 500, exact number unknown

The order of the Cleansing Flame is the main Order of the [[Church of the Righteous Sun|Church]], holding the highest titles and administrating the land currently held by the Church. It is lead by the Head Arbiter, who is also the Head of the Church itself. Since the start of the Civil War it has seen a large increase of numbers.
