Name: The Order of the Purifying Flame 
Affiliations: [[Prenntic Empire]]
Grandmaster: X
Members: around 200, exact number unknown

The Order of the Purifying Flame always believed that the [[Church of the Righteous Sun|Church]] should rule [[Prennso]] under a more centralized manner. This brought it the ire of the Head Arbiter and [[the Justicar]] himself. As such, they were always a bit closer to [[The Apprentice]] than the Church.

With the start of the Civil War, they have fully broken off with the Church and sided with the Prenntic Empire, in the hopes of unifying Prennso under a centralized state, just as they desired from the start. They are used by the Apprentice as an elite unit, put into use when something needs to be done right the first time, aswell as covert missions.
Naturally they are hated and deemed as heretics by the Church.
