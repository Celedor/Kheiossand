Far above the other realms of Kheiossand, oftentimes found somewhere over [[Aupuni Nalu]].
Only inhabited by [[Avians]]  and has been purely myth until very recently

They are heavily influenced by the Incas, especially in their aesthetics. They also have influences from the Sapmi and Himalayan cultures.

Created during the [[Upheaval of the Wings]] 

As the Dislodged Isles are about 5km above ground, there is barely to no contact between the Avians and the other races. While the Avians can fly, they are not enduring enough to go one way, yet alone two. 
The individual islands are relatively close together and are connected through bridges alone, but many Avians also fly above them. if the bridges and ropes would be cut, the isles could be steered apart from each other.

At this point the Dislodged Isles are as much a land of myth to the ground-dwellers as the Ground is for the Avians. The Avians aren't really united and more live under multiple tribes, each led by a tribal leader. The biggest isle houses the mummified corpse of [[The Unapproachable Wing]] and houses the council of the tribes if needed, but it hasn't been necessary for generations.
Given their altitude, the Avians believe they are the chosen people and believe themselves to be untouchable, something that will be changed soon with the advancement of technology by the ground-dwellers.
In reverence to their sire, deceased tribal leaders will also get mummified at their death and will also be treated as if they are still alive, attending parties and dinner. This does tend to create complications after a few generations and a change of ruling dynasties is not unheard of. Once that happens, the previously deceased tribal leaders will still be kept, but they are now treated as "officially" deceased and will not be treated as living anymore.
